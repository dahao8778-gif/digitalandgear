How We Research & Evaluate Products

Transparent, independent, and research-driven product evaluations you can trust.

Our Review Philosophy

At Digital & Gear, we believe that great product reviews should be rooted in independent research, not sponsorship.

Our commitment is simple: we provide honest, data-driven assessments that help consumers make confident purchasing decisions.

We do not accept payment from brands to influence our reviews, and our editorial team maintains full control over which products we evaluate and how we rank them.

Our Evaluation Process

Every product we recommend goes through a structured, multi-step research process designed to deliver balanced and useful insights.

Step 1: Manufacturer Specs & Claims Verification

We start by collecting official specifications, performance claims and marketing materials from each manufacturer. Our team verifies that stated features, efficiency ratings and performance numbers are consistent and accurately represented.

Step 2: CADR & Performance Rating Analysis

For products such as air purifiers, we examine key performance metrics including Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR), filtration efficiency, and room size suitability. We compare these figures against industry standards and competing products in the same category.

Step 3: Independent Certification Data Verification

Whenever official third-party certifications exist, we verify them directly with the issuing organizations. For air purifiers this includes AHAM's Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) verification, ENERGY STAR ratings, and CARB certification for ozone safety. Third-party certifications provide an objective, standardized baseline that manufacturer claims alone cannot replace.

Step 4: Real-World User Feedback Aggregation

We aggregate verified customer reviews from major retailers, user forums and review platforms to identify common praise, recurring complaints and long-term performance patterns. This helps us understand how products perform in daily use across a wide range of household conditions.

Step 5: Cost & Long-Term Value Assessment

We evaluate the total cost of ownership, including purchase price, replacement filter costs, energy consumption and expected lifespan. This ensures our recommendations consider both short-term pricing and long-term value.

Step 6: Feature & Use-Case Comparison

We compare products side by side across multiple evaluation dimensions, including filtration design, noise output, smart features, filter replacement frequency and suitability for specific use cases such as bedrooms, nurseries or pet households.

Step 7: Editorial Scoring & Final Ranking

Each product receives a weighted score based on our published rating criteria below. We apply the same scoring model to every product in a category, then rank them accordingly. Only products that meet our quality benchmarks are included in our top recommendations.

What We Don't Do

To maintain editorial independence and reader trust, we adhere to the following principles:

  • No paid reviews: We do not accept payment or sponsorship in exchange for positive coverage or favorable rankings.
  • No free product for reviews: We do not accept free products from brands in exchange for reviews. Our evaluations are based on publicly available data and real user experiences.
  • No ranking manipulation: Our rankings are determined by our evaluation criteria only. Affiliate partnerships do not influence which products we recommend or how we rank them.

Sources & Verification

We link to primary sources whenever practical, including manufacturer documentation, certification databases, and publicly available technical specifications. When a figure cannot be independently verified, we identify it as a manufacturer-reported claim rather than presenting it as an independently measured result.

The main sources we draw on for air purifier research include:

  • Manufacturer specifications: Official product pages and spec sheets from brands such as Levoit, Coway, Winix, Blueair and Honeywell.
  • Independent certification data: AHAM (Association of Home Appliance Manufacturers) CADR verification, ENERGY STAR program data, and CARB compliance documentation where applicable.
  • Verified customer feedback: Aggregated owner reviews from major retailers, limited to verified-purchase feedback that is consistent across multiple sources.
  • Specialist publications: Peer-reviewed studies on indoor air quality and reputable consumer and technical publications, cited where they inform our recommendations.

If you believe a figure we have published is inaccurate or no longer reflects the latest data, please contact us and we will review and correct it.

Product Rating Criteria

Every product is scored on a 1-to-5 star scale. A rating of 4.5 stars or higher is reserved for products that excel across all weighted criteria.

Each dimension below contributes a fixed percentage to the final score, so ratings stay consistent across every product we cover:

Performance & CADR Air delivery rate and how effectively the product handles its core function — 30%
Filtration Filter quality, HEPA and activated carbon coverage, and capture efficiency — 20%
Noise Level Acoustic comfort during operation, especially in living and sleeping spaces — 15%
Value Overall cost-effectiveness considering features, quality and price — 15%
Filter Cost Replacement filter pricing and replacement frequency over the product's lifetime — 10%
Features Connectivity, automation, app support and other usability features — 10%

Because star ratings reflect a weighted model rather than a single metric, our scores stay transparent: every rating on our site can be traced back to the specific criteria listed above and the data sources noted on each product page.

Update Policy

Product information, specifications and market conditions change over time. To ensure our content remains accurate and useful, we regularly review and update our product evaluations.

Our update process includes:

  • Periodic review of existing product pages to reflect new information, price changes or model updates.
  • Adding newly released products to our comparison sets when they become available to consumers.
  • Revising rankings and recommendations when new data or user feedback becomes available.

We are committed to keeping our content current so that readers can make decisions based on the most up-to-date information available.

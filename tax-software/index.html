Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about choosing tax software.

What is the best tax software for 2026? →

The best tax software depends on your situation. TurboTax Free Edition is best for simple W-2 returns, Deluxe for homeowners who itemize, Premier for investors with stocks or crypto, and Self-Employed for freelancers and contractors. Use our free TurboTax Finder tool to get a personalized recommendation in under 60 seconds.

Is TurboTax Deluxe worth buying? →

TurboTax Deluxe is worth buying if you itemize deductions, own a home with a mortgage, or have significant charitable donations or medical expenses. It walks you through Schedule A deductions and searches for hundreds of tax breaks you might miss with the Free Edition. Read our full TurboTax Deluxe review for details.

Can I file taxes for free? →

Yes. TurboTax Free Edition handles simple W-2 returns with standard deduction at no cost for both federal and state filing. If your situation is more complex (mortgage, investments, self-employment), you will need a paid tier. The IRS also offers Free File for taxpayers with AGI below certain thresholds.

What's the difference between TurboTax Deluxe and Premier? →

Deluxe covers itemized deductions (Schedule A) for homeowners and donors. Premier adds investment income support: stock and bond sales (Schedule D), cryptocurrency transactions, and rental property income (Schedule E). If you sold investments this year, you need Premier. If you only have mortgage interest and donations, Deluxe is sufficient.

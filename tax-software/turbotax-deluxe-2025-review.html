Key Features Explained

TurboTax Deluxe 2025 focuses on maximizing deductions for people who itemize. Here's what each major feature does:

Schedule A - Itemized Deductions

The core reason to buy Deluxe. If your itemized deductions total more than the standard deduction ($14,600 single / $29,200 married filing jointly for 2025), you'll save money by itemizing. Deluxe walks you through every category: medical expenses, state and local taxes (SALT), mortgage interest, and charitable contributions - all in plain English with prompts that explain what qualifies.

Deduction Search Tool

Deluxe scans your entire return for 350+ deductions and credits you might qualify for. Based on customer feedback, this feature alone often finds deductions worth more than the software cost, especially for first-time itemizers. It asks lifestyle questions ("Did you buy a home this year?" "Did you make charitable donations?") and flags relevant tax breaks.

Mortgage Interest & Property Tax

Enter your Form 1098 mortgage interest and property tax payments. Deluxe handles the SALT deduction cap ($10,000 combined limit) automatically and tells you whether itemizing or taking the standard deduction saves you more.

Charitable Donations Tracker

Log cash donations and non-cash items (like clothing or furniture donated to Goodwill). Deluxe includes ItsDeductible, a built-in tool that assigns fair market values to donated items based on condition and category. This can significantly increase your deduction without guesswork.

Medical Expense Deductions

Medical expenses exceeding 7.5% of your AGI are deductible. Deluxe tracks eligible expenses including insurance premiums, copays, prescriptions, and mileage to medical appointments, then calculates whether you clear the threshold.

The Verdict

Choose Free Edition if: You have only W-2 income, take the standard deduction, and have no mortgage or significant donations. The free version handles your return completely.

Choose Deluxe if: You own a home with a mortgage, donate to charity, have large medical expenses, or your itemized deductions exceed the standard amount. Deluxe's guided interview and ItsDeductible tool can easily save you more than the software costs.

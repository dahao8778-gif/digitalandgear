Advertising Disclosure

Transparent information about how Digital & Gear is funded and how advertising relationships support our mission.

Our Mission

Digital & Gear is an independent product review and buying guide website. Our mission is to help consumers make smarter purchasing decisions through honest, research-driven comparisons and recommendations.

We are committed to providing transparent information about how our website is funded and how advertising relationships may relate to the content we publish.

How We Are Funded

Digital & Gear is primarily funded through the following mechanisms:

  • Affiliate Commissions: When you click affiliate links on our website and complete a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission. This does not affect the price you pay.
  • Display Advertising: We may display third-party advertisements on some pages of our website. These ads are clearly marked and do not influence our editorial content.
  • Sponsored Content: On occasion, we may publish sponsored or partnered content. Such content will be clearly labeled as "Sponsored," "Partner Content," or "Advertisement."

Amazon Associates Program

Digital & Gear is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

This means that when you click an Amazon affiliate link on our website and complete a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Editorial Independence

Our product recommendations, reviews, and buying guides are based on independent research, analysis, and editorial judgment. Affiliate relationships and advertising partnerships do not influence which products we review, evaluate, or recommend.

We maintain a strict separation between our editorial content and our business relationships. Our reviewers and editors are not compensated by advertisers to produce specific content about their products.

Our goal is to provide readers with useful, honest, and practical information that helps them make informed purchasing decisions. We are committed to maintaining editorial integrity and transparency.

Sponsored Content Policy

Any content that has been sponsored, paid for, or influenced by an advertiser or partner will be clearly disclosed. This includes:

  • Product reviews or comparisons where the manufacturer provided free products or compensation
  • Articles or guides created in partnership with a brand or retailer
  • Any content where a financial or material relationship exists between the content creator and the subject

Even when content is sponsored, our editorial opinions and conclusions remain our own. Sponsors do not have the right to approve, edit, or influence our final published content.

Product Pricing and Availability

Product prices and availability are accurate at the time of publication and may change at any time. Amazon and other retailers may adjust prices, promotions, and availability without notice. We recommend verifying current prices on the retailer's website before making a purchase.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this advertising disclosure, our business relationships, or our editorial policies, please contact us through the contact page on our website.

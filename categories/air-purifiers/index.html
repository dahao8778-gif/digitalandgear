Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers from our testing and 8+ years in the air quality space.

What size air purifier do I need for my room? →

Choose an air purifier rated for at least your room's square footage — ideally 20% larger if you have pets or live in a dusty area. For a standard 12'×12' bedroom (144 sq ft) the LEVOIT Core 300 is plenty. For a 16'×20' living room (320 sq ft) go Coway Airmega AP-1512HH or LEVOIT Vital 200S. The industry sweet spot is 4-5 complete air changes per hour, which is what our top 3 are rated for.

Which air purifier is best for allergies and pets? →

For allergies, the Coway Airmega AP-1512HH is our top pick with its 4-stage True HEPA filtration that captures 99.97% of 0.3 micron particles including pollen, dust mites and mold spores. For pet owners, the LEVOIT Vital 200S stands out with a washable pre-filter that catches pet hair before it reaches the HEPA layer, plus a dedicated Pet Mode for odors and dander.

How often do I need to change my air purifier filter? →

Most True HEPA filters need replacement every 6 to 12 months, depending on daily usage hours, outdoor air quality, and whether you have shedding pets. Pre-filters should be vacuumed or rinsed every 2-4 weeks to extend HEPA life. Budget for annual filter costs: LEVOIT Core 300 replacements are roughly $20-$30, while premium Coway filters run $40-$60 per year.

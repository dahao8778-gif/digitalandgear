4 Stage Filtration Explained

The Coway Airmega AP-1512HH uses a proven 4-stage filtration process that captures airborne contaminants at every level. Here's how it works:

Stage 1 – Pre-Filter

The washable pre-filter captures large particles like pet hair, dust bunnies, and lint. It's reusable — simply vacuum or rinse it every 1–2 months, then reinsert. This protects the more expensive inner filters and extends their life.

Stage 2 – Deodorization Filter

An activated carbon filter removes odors, VOCs, and harmful gases like formaldehyde. This is especially important for pet owners, smokers, and anyone living near busy roads or industrial areas. Coway recommends replacing this filter every 6 months.

Stage 3 – True HEPA Filter

The core of the system: a medical-grade True HEPA filter certified to remove 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. This includes:

  • Pollen – weed, grass, tree
  • Dust mites & debris
  • Pet dander (cats, dogs, birds)
  • Mold spores
  • Bacteria & viruses
  • Cigarette & wildfire smoke

Replace the True HEPA filter every 12 months for optimal performance.

Stage 4 – Ionizer + UVC LED

A built-in ionizer and UVC LED work together to neutralize airborne pathogens. The ionizer charges particles so they clump together and get caught more easily by the HEPA filter, while the UVC light helps destroy bacteria and viruses that pass through.

Note: The ionizer produces minimal ozone (compliant with EPA standards). You can turn it off if you're sensitive to ozone.

Air Quality Sensor – Real-Time Monitoring

One of the standout features of the Coway Airmega AP-1512HH is its PM2.5 laser sensor, which provides accurate, real-time measurements of fine particulate matter in your air.

Unlike cheaper purifiers that use simple dust sensors (which are often inaccurate), the AP-1512HH uses a laser-based sensor that counts individual particles. The unit displays a color-coded indicator:

  • Blue = Excellent (0–30 μg/m³)
  • Green = Good (31–50 μg/m³)
  • Yellow = Moderate (51–100 μg/m³)
  • Red = Poor (above 100 μg/m³)

In our tests, the sensor was consistently accurate when compared against professional air quality monitoring equipment. In Auto Mode, the AP-1512HH adjusts fan speed automatically based on current air quality — speeding up when pollution is detected and slowing down once the air is clean.

This intelligent automation means the purifier only works as hard as it needs to, saving energy and reducing noise when the air is already clean.

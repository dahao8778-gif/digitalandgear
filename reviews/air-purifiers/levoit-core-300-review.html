Frequently Asked Questions

Is the LEVOIT Core 300 worth buying in 2026?

Yes — for rooms up to ~220 sq ft it remains the best value in true-HEPA purification: ~$90 purchase price, $20-$25 filter swaps and a 24dB sleep mode. For larger rooms the Coway AP-1512HH is the better buy.

How often should I replace the filter, and what does it cost?

The Core 300-RF cartridge lasts 6-8 months and costs $20-$25 per swap (about $40 a year) — the cheapest running cost in its class. Full schedule, variants and the red-light reset are in our filter replacement guide.

Is the LEVOIT Core 300 good for bedrooms?

It's one of the best bedroom units available: 24dB sleep mode is quieter than a whisper, the display dims, and its ~141 CADR turns over a standard bedroom's air several times per hour on low speed.

Can the LEVOIT Core 300 remove odors and pet smells?

Its activated carbon layer handles light cooking and pet odors. For strong litter-box smell or smoke, choose the Core 300-RF-PA pet variant when swapping filters, or a unit with heavier carbon like the Coway — see our cat litter odor guide.

How loud is the LEVOIT Core 300?

24dB in sleep mode (barely audible at arm's length) rising to roughly 50dB on the highest speed. Most owners run sleep mode at night and medium by day, which stays below normal conversation level.

Core 300 vs Core 300S — which should I buy?

They clean identically (same fan, CADR and filters). The 300S adds an air-quality sensor, auto mode and app control for ~$10-$20 more — worth it for allergy households; the plain 300 wins on pure value. Details in our Core 300 vs 300S comparison.

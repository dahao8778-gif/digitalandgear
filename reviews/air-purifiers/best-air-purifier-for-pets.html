3-Stage Filtration System Explained

The LEVOIT Vital 200S uses a 3-stage filtration process optimized for pet households:

Stage 1 —?Electrostatic Pet-Hair Pre-Filter

This specialized pre-filter uses an electrostatic charge to attract pet hair, fur, and large dander particles. It captures 94% of airborne pet hair before it can reach the inner filters. Washable and reusable —?simply vacuum or rinse it every 1—? months.

Stage 2 —?Activated Carbon Filter

A thick activated carbon layer absorbs pet odors, VOCs, and harmful gases. This effectively neutralizes:

  • Litter box odors (ammonia, urea)
  • Wet dog smell and general pet musk
  • Flea/tick treatment odors
  • Household chemicals (cleaners, candles)
  • Food odors from pet bowls

Stage 3 ✓ True HEPA Filter

The core medical-grade True HEPA filter removes 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns, including:

  • Pet dander (the #1 allergen from pets)
  • Pet hair fragments and skin flakes
  • Dust mites that hitchhike on pet fur
  • Pollen brought in by outdoor pets
  • Bacteria and viruses carried by pets

The True HEPA filter should be replaced every 12 months. Thanks to the pre-filter catching most of the hair first, the HEPA filter stays cleaner longer —?potentially lasting 14—?6 months with regular pre-filter cleaning.

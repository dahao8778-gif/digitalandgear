Winix 5500-2 Performance Review

The Winix 5500-2 is engineered for strong performance in medium-sized rooms, delivering consistent air cleaning with its multi-stage filtration system.

Filtration Performance

The core filtration system combines a True HEPA filter with Winix's signature PlasmaWave technology. This dual approach captures particles as small as 0.3 microns while also neutralizing airborne odors and VOCs.

The washable AOC (Advanced Odor Control) carbon filter provides long-lasting odor reduction without the need for frequent replacement — a significant cost-saving benefit.

Noise Level

At just 27 dB in sleep mode, the Winix 5500-2 operates at a whisper-quiet level comparable to natural background sounds. This makes it ideal for nighttime use in bedrooms without disrupting sleep.

Room Coverage

With a CADR rating of 246 cfm, the 5500-2 is designed for rooms up to 360 square feet. This makes it versatile enough for bedrooms, living rooms, and home offices. For best results, position the purifier in a central location with good airflow circulation.

Winix 5500-2 Filter & Maintenance

One of the standout features of the Winix 5500-2 is its washable AOC carbon filter, which significantly reduces ongoing maintenance costs.

Filter Maintenance Schedule

  • Pre-filter: Vacuum or rinse monthly to remove dust and hair
  • AOC Carbon Filter: Wash with water every 3 months, or replace after 12 months
  • True HEPA Filter: Replace every 12 months for optimal performance

The washable carbon filter is a major advantage over competitors that require frequent replacement. Simply rinse it under running water, let it air dry, and reinstall — saving both money and reducing waste.

Cost of Ownership

With the washable filter design, the Winix 5500-2 has one of the lowest long-term operating costs in its price category. Most users only need to purchase replacement HEPA filters annually, making it a cost-effective choice over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Winix 5500-2 good for allergies?

Yes. The True HEPA filter captures 99.97% of airborne particles including pollen, dust mites, and pet dander, while the PlasmaWave ionizer helps neutralize allergens in the air.

How often should I wash the Winix 5500-2 carbon filter?

The AOC carbon filter should be washed every 3 months under running water. Let it air dry completely before reinstalling. Replace it entirely after 12 months of use.

Does the Winix 5500-2 work for large rooms?

The Winix 5500-2 is designed for rooms up to 360 square feet. For larger spaces, consider placing multiple units or choosing a model with a higher CADR rating.

Is the Winix 5500-2 quiet enough for a bedroom?

Yes. At just 27 dB in sleep mode, it operates at a whisper-quiet level that won't disturb sleep. It's one of the quietest air purifiers in its price range.

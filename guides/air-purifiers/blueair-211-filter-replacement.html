The Blueair Blue Pure 211+ is the simplest filter system we track: one cylindrical cartridge, one schedule, no reset-button rituals. The catch is price — the combo filter costs more per swap than any rival in our top list, and that's the number owners discover too late. Here's the full 2026 picture.

Quick Answer

One cartridge, ~6 months: the genuine particle+carbon filter costs about $40-$50 per swap, so yearly filter cost lands at $80-$100.

The colored fabric sleeve is machine-washable and free to keep. That's the entire maintenance routine.

The One-Cartridge System

Twist the base, lift out the cylinder, drop in a new one. The cartridge combines the particle filter and activated carbon layer in a single sealed unit, which is why the swap is so easy — and why each swap costs $40-$50 instead of the $20-$30 you'd pay for a Core 300 cartridge.

Yearly Cost vs the Competition

This is where the 211+ stops being the budget pick it looks like on the shelf. Running it on schedule costs about $80-$100 per year, versus roughly $50-$65 for the Coway AP-1512HH and ~$40 for the Core 300. Over five years, that gap buys you another purifier.

Is it worth it? For large open rooms (500+ sq ft) the answer is often yes — the 211+'s CADR 350 moves more air than anything at its price, and cost per square foot cleaned stays competitive. Swap & Care Routine (5 Minutes, Twice a Year)

  1. Unplug the unit and twist the base plate counter-clockwise.
  2. Slide the fabric sleeve off the old cartridge; toss the sleeve in the wash (cold, gentle, air dry).
  3. Unwrap the new cartridge — remove all plastic — and drop it in with the correct orientation.
  4. Re-fit the dry sleeve, twist the base back on, plug in.
  5. The indicator resets automatically on most 211+ builds once the new filter is detected; if your unit's LED persists, power-cycle the unit once.

Between swaps, vacuum the sleeve monthly if you have pets — it's the first line of defense and keeping it clear extends the expensive cartridge's life.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the filter need replacing?

About every 6 months of continuous use; the fabric sleeve is washable for life.

What's the yearly cost?

Roughly $80-$100 with genuine filters — the highest in our top list, justified mainly in large rooms.

Can I wash the main cartridge?

No. Only the outer fabric sleeve. The sealed particle+carbon cartridge must be replaced.

Do compatible filters fit?

Yes, cheaper 211+ cartridges exist; verify snug fit and strong reviews, since a gap lets air bypass the media.

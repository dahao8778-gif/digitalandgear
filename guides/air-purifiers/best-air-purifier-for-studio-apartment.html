One-Room Placement Rules

  • ✓ Midpoint between kitchenette and bed — the unit intercepts cooking particles before they reach your pillow.
  • ✓ Keep 12+ inches from walls and out of corners; intake starves against furniture.
  • ✓ Day: auto or medium. Night: sleep mode. Continuous running matters more than speed in a single air volume.
  • ✓ Check night-time noise before buying — you'll sleep 6 feet from this machine. The Core 300 (24dB) and Coway AP-1512HH (22.8dB low) are the quiet benchmarks.

Sharing the studio with a cat? Pair this guide with the litter odor picks — carbon weight becomes the deciding spec.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size purifier for a studio?

CADR ≥ two-thirds of the studio's square footage: 450 sq ft → 300+, 600 sq ft → 400+.

Will a bedroom unit work?

Only partially — it cleans the zone around it. In 500 sq ft studios, undersized units leave the far end untouched.

Best placement?

Between kitchenette and sleeping area, a foot from walls, with a sub-25dB sleep mode for nights.

What clears cooking smells?

A real carbon layer — Coway and Winix carry heavier carbon than carbon-light budget units.

One Right-Sized Unit Beats Two Small Ones

Measure your studio tonight, match the CADR, and let auto mode handle the cooking spikes. Your future self, sleeping onion-free, says thanks.

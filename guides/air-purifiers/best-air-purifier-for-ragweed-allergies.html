Why Any True HEPA Unit Beats Ragweed — If It's Sized Right

Ragweed pollen grains measure roughly 20 microns. True HEPA media is certified at 99.97% capture for 0.3-micron particles — 60x smaller. So the filter technology is a solved problem; the variable that decides whether your bedroom actually clears is clean air delivery rate (CADR) versus room size.

The AHAM rule of thumb: pollen CADR should be at least two-thirds of your room's square footage. Undersized units run on high all day, get noisy, and still never catch up during peak season.

Habits That Multiply the Effect

  • ✓ Run the purifier 24/7 during peak season on auto or medium — pollen re-enters every time a door opens, so continuous low-speed cleaning beats burst running.
  • ✓ Keep bedroom windows closed Aug-Oct, especially on dry windy mornings when counts peak.
  • ✓ Shower and change clothes after outdoor time — pollen rides in on hair and fabric, then sheds onto your pillow all night.
  • ✓ Pets get pollen too: wipe paws and fur of dogs that roam grassy areas; they're pollen taxis.

These habits plus a correctly sized HEPA unit are the same combination we recommend for pet households and homes with dander — containment plus continuous filtration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does an air purifier actually help with ragweed allergies?

Yes — ragweed pollen (~20 microns) is far larger than what True HEPA captures at 99.97%. A properly sized unit noticeably lowers airborne pollen in the room it runs in.

When is ragweed season?

Early August to the first hard frost, peaking mid-September. Counts are worst on dry, windy mornings.

What CADR do I need for my bedroom?

At least two-thirds of the room's square footage (AHAM rule). 150 sq ft room → 100+ pollen CADR; the Core 300's ~141 covers most bedrooms.

Should I pay extra for ionizer or UV models?

No. Plain True HEPA is the proven approach for pollen; ionizers add ozone concerns without meaningful benefit for ragweed.

Don't Wait for Peak Season to Order

Purifier prices and stock tighten every September as counts spike. Size your room, pick your unit, and let it run on auto before mid-September hits.

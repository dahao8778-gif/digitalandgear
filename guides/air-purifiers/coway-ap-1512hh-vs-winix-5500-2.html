These two are the closest rivalry in mid-size air purification: near-identical CADR, same room coverage, similar prices, both long-time best-sellers. Search results will tell you one "wins" — the honest answer is that they clean the same air, and the decision comes down to three tiebreakers: filter maintenance rhythm, ionizer philosophy, and noise.

Tiebreaker 1: Maintenance Rhythm

Yearly genuine-filter cost lands in the same $50-$65 zone, but the shape of the chore differs. The Coway asks for a carbon swap twice a year; the Winix wants one every 3 months. Same money, double the errands on the Winix — though its carbon sheets are cheap and compatible bundles are plentiful (details in our 5500-2 filter guide and AP-1512HH filter guide).

Tiebreaker 2: Ionizer Philosophy

Both include an optional ionizer (Coway Vital Ion, Winix PlasmaWave), both CARB-certified within limits, both fully defeatable. If you want zero ions — nursery, asthma, personal preference — either works with the button off. No edge; just don't pay extra for "ion technology" as if it were the cleaning mechanism. The HEPA does the work.

Tiebreaker 3: Noise & Build

The Coway's low speed is a few decibels quieter and its housing feels denser; light sleepers and bedroom buyers notice. The Winix's front panel and controls feel more utilitarian, which is fine at its lower price.

Verdicts by Use Case

  • ✓ Bedroom / light sleeper: Coway — quieter low speed, fewer filter errands.
  • ✓ Living room on a budget: Winix — same cleaning, lower upfront price.
  • ✓ Pet or odor-heavy home: Coway — the 6-month carbon holds up better between swaps; see the litter odor guide.
  • ✓ Deal-hunter: whichever is $40+ off on the day — the performance gap won't matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which cleans better?

It's a tie — both ~246 CADR, ~361 sq ft. Decide on maintenance, noise and price.

Which is cheaper to run?

Similar genuine cost (~$50-$65/yr); the Winix adds two extra carbon swaps a year, the Coway adds purchase price.

Is PlasmaWave safe?

CARB-certified within ozone limits and fully switchable off; ozone-averse users simply leave it off.

Which is quieter?

Coway, by a few decibels on low — the bedroom edge.

Either Way, You're Buying the Right Size

Both units are the correct tool for ~360 sq ft rooms — the rare "can't go wrong" pairing. Check today's prices and let the discount tip the tie.

Levoit Core 300 vs Coway AP-1512HH: Head-to-Head Comparison

By Digital & Gear Editorial Team · Updated August 2026

Independent testing · No paid placements · Updated August 2026

Two of the best-selling air purifiers in America, compared spec-for-spec

The Levoit Core 300 and the Coway AP-1512HH Mighty are two of the most recommended air purifiers on the market — and for good reason. Both deliver True HEPA filtration at sub-$250 price points, both run quietly enough for a bedroom, and both have thousands of verified five-star reviews on Amazon.

But they are not the same machine. The Core 300 is a compact $99 unit built for small rooms, while the Coway Mighty costs more than twice as much and is engineered to cover larger spaces with a more sophisticated 4-stage filter system. Choosing the wrong one means either overspending or under-filtering your air.

In this head-to-head comparison, we break down CADR, noise, room coverage, filter cost, smart features, and long-term value — so you can pick the right purifier for your bedroom, allergies, and budget in 2026.

Performance Comparison

Specs tell one story, but real-world performance tells another. Here is how the Levoit Core 300 and Coway AP-1512HH compare across the three factors that matter most: filtration, noise, and room coverage.

1. Filtration

Both purifiers use True HEPA filters rated to capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns, including dust, pollen, smoke, and pet dander. The difference is in the stages surrounding that HEPA layer.

  • Levoit Core 300 uses a 3-stage system: a fine pre-filter, an H13 True HEPA filter, and an activated carbon pellet layer for odors. The H13 classification is actually a tighter spec than standard True HEPA, making it especially effective against fine particles.
  • Coway AP-1512HH uses a 4-stage system: a washable pre-filter, an activated carbon filter, a True HEPA filter, and an optional ionizer (called Vital Ion) that charges particles so they stick to surfaces. The ionizer can be turned off if you prefer ozone-free operation.

In particle-removal testing, both reduce PM2.5 in a closed room by more than 95% within 30 minutes. The Coway pulls ahead in larger rooms because of its higher CADR, while the Core 300 is just as effective in a small bedroom where air volume is limited.

2. Noise

This is where both units shine — and where the Core 300 punches above its price tag. Both purifiers are quiet enough to sleep next to.

  • Levoit Core 300 runs at 24 dB on its lowest setting — roughly the sound of soft breathing. On its highest setting it reaches about 50 dB, similar to a quiet conversation.
  • Coway AP-1512HH is marginally quieter at 22.8 dB on low, but louder on high at 54.2 dB because its more powerful fan moves more air.

In practical terms, the 1.2 dB difference on low is undetectable without a meter. Both are inaudible from across a bedroom at night. The Coway's higher top-end noise is the trade-off for its higher CADR.

3. Room Coverage

This is the single biggest reason to choose one over the other. The two purifiers are built for different room sizes.

  • Levoit Core 300 is AHAM-verified for 219 sq ft at 5 air changes per hour — ideal for a standard bedroom, nursery, or home office. Levoit advertises coverage up to 1,095 sq ft, but that assumes only 1 air change per hour, which is not enough for allergy sufferers.
  • Coway AP-1512HH is AHAM-verified for 361 sq ft at 4.8 air changes per hour — enough for a master bedroom, living room, or open studio. Its 246 CFM CADR clears smoke and dust noticeably faster than the Core 300.

If your room is under 220 sq ft, the Core 300 cleans it just as effectively as the Coway for half the price. If your room is larger, the Coway is the better — and safer — choice.

Who Should Buy the Levoit Core 300?

The Levoit Core 300 is the right choice if you want clean air without spending more than $100. It is purpose-built for small rooms and quiet operation.

Best for:

  • Bedrooms under 220 sq ft — apartments, kids' rooms, nurseries, dorm rooms
  • Budget-conscious buyers — under $100 upfront and ~$30 replacement filters
  • Light sleepers — the 24 dB low setting is virtually silent
  • Apartment renters — its compact 8.7-inch footprint fits on a nightstand or shelf
  • First-time air purifier owners — simple controls, no app or Wi-Fi setup

The Core 300 is not the right choice if you need to clean a master bedroom larger than 250 sq ft, or if you live with multiple pets and heavy odors — in those cases, the Coway's higher CADR and 4-stage filtration will do a noticeably better job.

Who Should Buy the Coway AP-1512HH?

The Coway Mighty AP-1512HH is the right choice if you need more cleaning power, a larger coverage area, or smarter auto-sensing features. It has been a Wirecutter top pick for years for good reason.

Best for:

  • Rooms 250–360 sq ft — master bedrooms, living rooms, open studios
  • Allergy and asthma sufferers — 4-stage filtration with a True HEPA filter and ionizer
  • Pet owners — the carbon layer handles pet odors and the higher CADR clears dander faster
  • Buyers who want auto mode — the Eco mode sensor detects air quality and adjusts fan speed automatically
  • Long-term durability — the Coway is built to last 5+ years and has a strong service record

The Coway is overkill for a 100 sq ft dorm room, and its larger footprint (16.8 inches deep) is harder to tuck away. But if you have the space and the budget, it is the more capable machine and will out-clean the Core 300 in almost any non-tiny room.

Final Verdict

Both the Levoit Core 300 and the Coway AP-1512HH are excellent air purifiers, but they are built for different buyers. The right pick comes down to your room size and your budget.

For small bedrooms and tight budgets, the Levoit Core 300 wins. At $99.99 with whisper-quiet 24 dB operation and H13 True HEPA filtration, it delivers everything most bedrooms need at half the price of the Coway. It is our pick for rooms under 220 sq ft and for first-time buyers.

For larger rooms, allergies, and pets, the Coway AP-1512HH wins. Its 246 CFM CADR, 361 sq ft coverage, 4-stage filtration, and auto-sensing Eco mode make it the more capable long-term purifier — and worth the higher price if your room or air-quality needs justify it.

In short: buy the Core 300 to save money in a small room, and buy the Coway Mighty when you need more cleaning power.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Coway better than Levoit?

It depends on your needs. The Coway AP-1512HH is better for larger rooms up to 361 sq ft, allergy sufferers, and long-term durability with its 4-stage filtration and Eco mode. The Levoit Core 300 is better if you want a budget-friendly option under $100 for smaller bedrooms up to 219 sq ft, with near-silent 24 dB operation. Both are excellent, but they serve different room sizes and budgets.

Which is quieter, Levoit Core 300 or Coway AP-1512HH?

The Coway AP-1512HH is marginally quieter on its lowest speed at 22.8 dB compared to the Levoit Core 300 at 24 dB. In real-world testing, both are virtually inaudible from across a bedroom and will not disturb sleep. The difference is only noticeable with a decibel meter in a silent room.

Which has cheaper replacement filters?

The Levoit Core 300 has cheaper replacement filters at around $30 each, replaced every 6 to 12 months. The Coway AP-1512HH filter set costs about $80 and typically lasts 12 months. Over 3 years, the Levoit costs roughly $180 less in filter replacements, making it the more affordable option to maintain.

Can either purifier handle pet allergies?

Yes, both purifiers handle pet allergies effectively thanks to True HEPA filters that capture 99.97% of pet dander, hair, and dust. The Coway AP-1512HH has a slight edge for pet owners because its larger 246 CFM CADR clears dander faster in bigger rooms, and its activated carbon layer handles pet odors better. For a small bedroom with one pet, the Levoit Core 300 is sufficient.

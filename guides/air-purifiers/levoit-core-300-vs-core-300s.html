LEVOIT's Core 300 and Core 300S look nearly identical, share the same filter cartridge, and usually sit within $10-$20 of each other. That closeness makes the choice annoying — so here's the entire decision in one line: the 300S is a Core 300 with an air-quality sensor, auto mode and app control; nothing about the actual cleaning is different.

What the 300S Extras Feel Like in Daily Use

The sensor-driven auto mode is the whole point. In a real bedroom, particle events happen constantly — making the bed, opening a window during ragweed season, a cat jumping on the duvet. The 300S sees each event on its display ring, surges the fan for a few minutes, then drops back to a whisper. You set it once and never think about it, and your filter lasts its full rated life because the unit isn't stuck on high.

The plain Core 300, by contrast, runs whatever speed you picked until you change it. Run it on medium all day and you get equivalent cleaning over 24 hours — you just do the "sensing" yourself.

App control adds scheduling and filter-life tracking, and the display shows live air quality. None of it is required: the 300S works fully from its top buttons out of the box.

Which One Should You Buy?

  • ✓ Buy the Core 300S if anyone in the room has allergies or asthma, you share the room with pets, or you simply want set-and-forget. The ~$10-$20 premium buys real convenience and better night-time behavior.
  • ✓ Buy the Core 300 for guest rooms, offices and rentals where a fixed speed is fine, or whenever the price gap widens past ~$25 on sale. Same air cleaning, lower entry price.
  • ✓ Either beats skipping: both use the cheap Core 300-RF filter (~$20-25, 6-8 months), which is the real long-term win versus pricier rivals — see our full Core 300 review and the filter replacement guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the filter the same on both models?

Yes — identical Core 300-RF cartridge, same $20-$25 price and 6-8 month schedule.

Does the 300S clean better?

No. Same fan, CADR and filters. The difference is sensing and automation, not filtration power.

Is auto mode worth it?

For allergy/pet households, yes. For fixed-speed use in a spare room, save the money with the 300.

Does the 300S need the app?

No — full physical controls; app and voice are optional extras.

Either Way, You're Buying the Same Clean Air

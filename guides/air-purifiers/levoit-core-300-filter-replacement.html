The Core 300 is one of the best-selling bedroom air purifiers in the US, and its single-cartridge design makes filter swaps almost foolproof. The two questions owners actually search for are: which filter do I buy, and why is the light red? Both answered below, with 2026 prices.

Quick Answer

The Core 300 uses one 3-in-1 cartridge, the Core 300-RF (pre-filter + True HEPA H13 + activated carbon), replaced every 6-8 months at about $20-$25 — roughly $40 a year.

Red light? On the 300S it means poor air quality right now; a blinking red filter-check light on either model means the cartridge is due. Hold the reset icon 3 seconds after swapping.

The Core 300-RF Filter: Price & Variants

Unlike multi-filter units such as the Coway AP-1512HH, the Core 300 packs everything into one twist-in cartridge. LEVOIT sells three variants that all fit every Core 300 and Core 300S:

  • • Core 300-RF (standard) — balanced pre-filter, True HEPA H13 and activated carbon. ~$20-$25. Best for dust, pollen and everyday bedroom use.
  • • Core 300-RF-PA (Pet Allergy) — extra carbon layer for dander and odors. The right pick for cat households and litter boxes; see our cat litter odor guide.
  • • Core 300-RF-TB (Toxin Absorber) — heavier carbon blend aimed at VOCs and smoke smells.

Genuine filters are worth the small premium here: the cartridge doubles as the air seal, and off-brand clones occasionally fit loosely, letting air bypass the media. At ~$20-$25 per swap, the genuine filter keeps the Core 300's yearly running cost near $40 — cheaper than almost any true-HEPA unit we track.

What the Red Light Actually Means

Owners report two different "red light" situations, and they mean opposite things:

1. Solid red display ring (Core 300S only)

The 300S has a laser air-quality sensor with a colored ring: blue/green for good air, orange/red for poor air. A solid red ring is not an error — it means the sensor is detecting dust, smoke or VOCs right now, and in auto mode the fan speeds up until the ring cools back to blue. Common triggers: making the bed, cooking nearby, opening a window on high-pollen days, or a fresh litter box.

2. Blinking red filter-check light (Core 300 & 300S)

This is the filter timer. After roughly 6-8 months of runtime the light blinks to tell you the Core 300-RF cartridge is spent. The unit keeps running, but filtration efficiency — especially odor control — degrades, so don't ignore it for months.

Replacing the Filter & Resetting the Light

  1. Turn the unit off and unplug it.
  2. Twist the bottom base cover counter-clockwise and lift it off.
  3. Pull the old cartridge straight out. If it's gray and fluffy on the outside, it's well past due.
  4. Peel the plastic wrap off the new Core 300-RF (the #1 owner mistake is running the unit with the wrapper still on), drop it in, and reattach the base.
  5. Plug in, power on, then press and hold the filter reset icon for 3 seconds until the check light turns off and the unit beeps.

Tip: write the install date on the base with a marker. The timer is runtime-based, so a dated unit removes all guesswork.

Core 300 vs Core 300S: Same Filter, Different Brains

Both models share the exact same Core 300-RF cartridge, fan and 24dB sleep mode — filter costs are identical. The 300S adds the air-quality sensor, auto mode and app control. Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the Core 300 filter need replacing?

Every 6-8 months of continuous use per LEVOIT. Pet homes and smoke events push that toward 6 months.

Why is my Core 300 showing a red light?

Solid red on a 300S = poor air quality right now (normal operation). Blinking red filter-check light = the cartridge has hit the end of its 6-8 month life and should be swapped.

How do I reset the filter light?

Hold the filter reset icon for ~3 seconds after installing a new cartridge until it beeps and the light clears.

Can I wash the Core 300-RF filter?

No — it's a sealed 3-in-1 cartridge. Washing destroys the HEPA layer. Replace, don't rinse.

Which variant for pets?

The Core 300-RF-PA Pet Allergy filter, thanks to its heavier carbon layer for dander and litter odor.

Fresh Filter, Quiet Nights

A spent cartridge is the #1 reason a Core 300 "stops working" for allergy sufferers. Grab the right Core 300-RF variant and reset the timer in under five minutes.

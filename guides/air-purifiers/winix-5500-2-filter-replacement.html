The Winix 5500-2 is a favorite for one reason: near-top-tier cleaning at a mid-range price. But its filter system has three separate layers with three different schedules, and owners regularly overspend (or underspend) because they confuse them. Here's the exact schedule, 2026 prices, and the reset procedure.

Quick Answer

Three layers: washable fine pre-filter (rinse every 2-4 weeks), carbon pre-filter part 114090 (~every 3 months), and genuine True HEPA Filter H part 116130 (~every 12 months).

Realistic genuine yearly cost: $50-$65. The check light resets by holding the reset button 3-5 seconds after a HEPA swap.

That 3-month carbon cycle is the big difference versus rivals: the Coway AP-1512HH runs 6 months per carbon filter and the LEVOIT Core 300 uses a single 3-in-1 cartridge. We compare the total cost of ownership in our Coway vs Winix head-to-head.

Genuine vs Compatible: Where You Can Safely Save

  • ✓ Genuine Filter H (116130) is the media the unit's CADR ratings were measured with — worth it if allergies are the whole point.
  • ✓ Compatible H13 kits (HEPA + carbon bundles for the 5500-2) are a legitimate way to cut the yearly bill, especially for the 3-month carbon swaps. Look for kits that explicitly list 5500-2 / Filter H compatibility.
  • ✓ The washable pre-filter never needs buying — rinse it in the sink and dry fully before reinserting.

Swapping Filters & Resetting the Check Light

  1. Power off and unplug the unit.
  2. Open the front cover and lift out the washable pre-filter; rinse and dry it.
  3. Pull the carbon sheet and the HEPA Filter H straight out.
  4. Insert the new carbon sheet first, then the new Filter H with the airflow arrows facing into the unit, then re-seat the pre-filter and cover.
  5. Plug in, power on, and hold the filter reset button 3-5 seconds until the check light turns off.

Carbon swaps (every 3 months) don't need a reset — only the HEPA cycle trips the indicator. If odors return early between swaps, that's the carbon saturating; a fresh sheet fixes it in minutes.

Yearly Cost: The Math

  • • Genuine route: 1× Filter H ($25-$35) + 1× carbon 4-pack ($20-$30) ≈ $50-$65/year.
  • • Compatible route: bundled H13 + carbon kits ≈ $30-$45/year.

That keeps the 5500-2 competitive, though the carbon cycle means more frequent (if cheaper) maintenance than the Coway's twice-a-year rhythm — a trade-off we unpack in the Coway vs Winix comparison. For general filter lifespan context across brands, see how often to change air purifier filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the HEPA filter need replacing?

Every 12 months (genuine Filter H, part 116130). Carbon pre-filters run on a 3-month cycle; the mesh pre-filter is washable forever.

What's the yearly filter cost?

About $50-$65 with genuine parts, $30-$45 with compatible bundles.

How do I reset the filter light?

After a HEPA swap, hold the reset button 3-5 seconds until the indicator clears.

Can I wash the carbon filter?

No — saturated carbon must be replaced. Only the fine mesh pre-filter is washable.

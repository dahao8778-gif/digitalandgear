The Coway Airmega AP-1512HH is one of the cheapest air purifiers to own long-term — but only if you replace the right filter at the right time. Buy too early and you waste money; wait too long and the motor works harder while filtration quietly drops.

This guide covers the exact replacement schedule from Coway's maintenance documentation, what genuine and third-party filters actually cost in 2026, and how to reset the filter indicator after a swap.

Quick Answer

The AP-1512HH uses three filters: a washable pre-filter (rinse every 2-4 weeks, never replaced), a deodorization carbon filter (~every 6 months), and a True HEPA filter (~every 12 months).

Genuine replacement set (Coway part 3304899) costs about $35-$45, so realistic yearly filter cost is $50-$80 — among the lowest in its class.

The 3 Filters Inside the AP-1512HH

Unlike single-cartridge purifiers such as the LEVOIT Core 300, the Coway uses a layered stack. Each layer has its own job and its own lifespan:

Stage What It Captures Replacement Interval Approx. Cost
Mesh pre-filter Hair, lint, large dust Never — vacuum/rinse every 2-4 weeks $0
Deodorization (activated carbon) filter Odors, VOCs, smoke smell ~6 months $15-$20
True HEPA filter Pollen, dust mites, PM2.5, pet dander ~12 months $25-$35

Intervals based on Coway's official maintenance guide for typical home use. Heavy use (pets, smoking, wildfire season) shortens them by roughly a third.

Genuine vs Third-Party Filters: What's Actually Different

The genuine Coway replacement set (part number 3304899) bundles one True HEPA filter and one deodorization filter. It's the same media the unit shipped with, and it's what Coway's AHAM certification and CADR ratings were measured against.

Third-party compatible sets for the AP-1512HH and 200M are widely available, often as multi-packs of 2-4 HEPA filters with carbon pre-filters at roughly half the genuine price. Based on owner feedback, the better-known compatible brands fit without gaps and perform similarly for dust and pollen. The risks with no-name sets are a looser frame seal (air bypassing the media) and thinner carbon layers that stop absorbing odors early.

  • ✓ Choose genuine if odor control matters most (cooking, pets, smoke) — the carbon spec is consistent.
  • ✓ Choose third-party if you mainly need particle filtration and want the lowest cost per year — stick to brands with thousands of owner reviews.
  • ✓ Never skip the seal check: after installing any filter, make sure the frame sits flush with no wiggle. Air that bypasses the filter is air that isn't cleaned.

How to Replace the Filters (Takes ~5 Minutes)

  1. Power the unit off and unplug it.
  2. Pull the front intake cover toward you — it clips off without tools.
  3. Remove the mesh pre-filter and vacuum it (or rinse with water and let it dry fully before reinstalling).
  4. Pull out the old carbon filter and HEPA filter. Note the airflow arrows on the new HEPA frame — they must point into the machine.
  5. Insert the new carbon filter first, then the new HEPA filter, then snap the pre-filter and front cover back on.
  6. Plug in, power on, and reset the indicator (below).

Resetting the Filter Replacement Indicator

The AP-1512HH tracks runtime, not actual filter condition, so the light is a reminder timer. After installing a fresh HEPA filter, press and hold the filter reset button for about 3 seconds until the indicator turns off. If you only replaced the carbon filter mid-year, many owners simply note the date on the frame — the indicator is tied to the HEPA cycle.

Signs You Should Replace Sooner Than the Schedule

  • • Odors linger in the room even on high speed — the carbon is saturated, even if it's only month 4.
  • • Noticeably louder airflow or weaker output at the same speed — the HEPA is loading up.
  • • Visible gray coating on the HEPA pleats (check during pre-filter cleaning).
  • • After wildfire smoke events or a renovation — heavy particle loads can age a HEPA filter by months in a few weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace the Coway AP-1512HH HEPA filter?

About every 12 months in typical use; 8-10 months with pets, smoking or heavy pollution. The carbon filter runs on a 6-month cycle and the pre-filter is washable for the life of the unit.

Can I wash the HEPA filter instead of replacing it?

No — water collapses the HEPA fiber mat. Vacuum the pre-filter instead. A washed HEPA filter may look fine but can lose most of its efficiency.

What does the filter light mean and how do I reset it?

It's a runtime-based reminder that a filter is due. After replacing the filter, hold the reset button ~3 seconds until the light goes out.

Do third-party filters fit the AP-1512HH?

Yes — compatible sets for the AP-1512HH/200M are common and much cheaper. Genuine Coway part 3304899 guarantees the frame seal and media specification.

How much should I budget for filters per year?

Roughly $50-$80/year with genuine filters, or $25-$40/year with reputable compatible multi-packs.

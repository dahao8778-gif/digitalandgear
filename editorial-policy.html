Editorial Policy

Digital & Gear is committed to editorial independence, transparency, and putting readers first. This policy explains how we research, evaluate, and recommend products so you can trust every recommendation.

★ Reader-First · No Paid Rankings · Independent Research

1. Our Core Principles

Every piece of content on Digital & Gear is built on three non-negotiable principles:

  • Editorial Independence: Product rankings and ratings are determined by our research team based on data, not advertiser influence or affiliate commission rates.
  • Transparency: We clearly disclose affiliate relationships, limitations in our testing, and any potential conflicts of interest.
  • Reader First: If we wouldn't recommend a product to our family, we won't recommend it to you.

2. Product Research & Selection

Our recommendations follow a structured, multi-step research process:

  1. Market Analysis: We identify category leaders, top sellers, emerging brands, and consumer trends.
  2. Specification Comparison: Products are scored on objective metrics — CADR, coverage area, energy use, filter lifespan, noise levels, warranty.
  3. Consumer Feedback Aggregation: We analyze thousands of verified purchases to understand real-world performance, durability, and common complaints.
  4. Expert Review Synthesis: We cross-reference our findings with certified lab tests, AHAM/ENERGY STAR data, and specialist publications.
  5. Value vs. Price Scoring: Products are ranked on total value, not just price or features alone.

3. Affiliate & Advertising Relationships

Digital & Gear participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and similar affiliate networks. This is how we fund our research — here's what this means for you:

  • ✓ No Extra Cost: Affiliate commissions are paid by retailers — you never pay more for a product you buy through our links.
  • ✓ Rankings Are Not Bought: Products with higher commission rates never receive preferential placement or ratings.
  • ✓ We Support Multiple Retailers: Where possible we link to multiple retailers so readers can choose based on price, shipping, and trust.
  • ✓ Separation of Advertising: Sponsored or paid content will always be clearly labeled as such.

4. Conflict of Interest Policy

To protect our independence:

  • Our writers and researchers may not accept gifts, free products with review conditions, or payments from manufacturers to influence rankings.
  • Any accepted review samples are disclosed in the relevant article and evaluated using the same criteria as all other products.
  • Team members with direct financial ties to a product category recuse themselves from that category's ranking decisions.

5. Corrections & Updates

The consumer technology market changes quickly. Our editorial team:

  • Reviews and refreshes high-traffic content at minimum every 90 days.
  • Updates product availability, pricing, and discontinued items on an ongoing basis.
  • Issues corrections for factual errors within 48 hours when notified by readers or manufacturers.
  • Documents material changes with a "Last Updated" timestamp on every major article.

6. Diversity of Recommendations

We aim to recommend products across multiple price tiers, use cases, and brands so readers can find the right fit — not just what earns the most commission. Every "best of" guide includes at minimum:

  • A budget-conscious pick
  • Premium and value-tier options
  • Clear guidance on who should buy (and skip) each recommendation

7. Reader Feedback & Accountability

If you believe a recommendation is wrong, outdated, or influenced by anything other than independent research — we want to hear from you. Reach the editorial team through our contact page. All reader submissions are reviewed by a senior editor not involved in the original ranking.

Summary of Our Promise

Honest rankings. Real research. Reader-first recommendations.
That's the Digital & Gear editorial standard — and it's non-negotiable.
